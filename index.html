Privacy Policy

3 Day Split · Effective October 18, 2026

3 Day Split does not collect, store, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads today's step count from Apple Health to show on rest days. The count is read on your iPhone, shown on your iPhone, and never leaves it. The app does not write to Apple Health and does not use Health data for advertising or share it with anyone.

Workout check-offs

The lifts you tick off are saved only on your device so the app and its widget can show them. They reset each day and are deleted when you delete the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
